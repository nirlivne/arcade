    How to play

    Pull back from anywhere, then let go.

    Knock down every tin. One shot per member.

    Drums take two hits. The first dents it, the second bursts it.

    Crates break open and drop what's on them.

    Hold the binoculars (or V) to peek at the fort before you aim.

    Stars come from your score, not who stayed home.

    About

    Your family's names and photos are saved only on this device. They are never uploaded, and they never appear in a share line.
