# Cannonball Family

Your family as a circus stunt troupe. Pick your people, load them into the felt cannon, and fire them at the
fairground tins and jam jars on the wooden ledges. The safety net is always there.

**Age band:** family 6+, from kids to grandparents. The demo and the how-to are pictures; no reading is needed to play.

## How to play

- **Pull back from anywhere, then let go** to fire. The dotted aim line shows the way.
- Knock down every tin on the level. One shot per family member.
- Pick the family in **Our family**: names, seats and looks are yours. Photos stay on this device only.
- The daily shot is the same level for everyone that day, with its own share line.

## Controls

- Mouse or touch: pull back anywhere and let go.
- Keyboard: Left / Right to aim, Up / Down for power, Space or Enter to fire.
- `R` restarts the level. `Esc` pauses; the game also pauses when you switch tabs.

## Comforts

Pause, mute, a how-to screen reachable at any time, instant retry, and a first-20-seconds safety net. Your
level progress, best scores, daily record and family are saved automatically on this device.

## Run locally

Cannonball Family's game code loads as ES modules, which Chrome blocks from a plain `file://` page. Serve the
folder with any static file server (for example `python -m http.server`) and open it from `http://` instead.
It works offline once loaded and can be installed as an app.
