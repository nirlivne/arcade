# Tray Disaster

Keep the tray steady. That's it. That's the job.

## How to play

A waiter carries a tray down the diner aisle, and the dishes just keep coming. Tilt the
tray to counter their wobble before the stack tips past the point of no return. Every
dish makes the next one twitchier, and the diner is not going to hold still for you.

- **Drag:** press and drag left or right anywhere on the screen. The tray tilts with your
  drag, not with where your finger happens to be — so you can drag from anywhere and keep going.
- **Keyboard:** Left/Right arrows or A/D tilt the tray.
- **R:** restart instantly, any time.

When the stack finally goes over, you get the bill: total damages, how many plates went
down, and whether the lobster made it out. Beat your best damages next round.
