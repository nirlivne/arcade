The giant trunk, boulder-line modules and goose (assets/trunk.glb, assets/boulders.glb, assets/goose.glb)

In-house, built by build/make_a12.py (Blender, headless Python; THE-1296, spec amendment A12).
Not derived from any third-party asset, CC0 library, or reference model.
Shapes are procedural (lofted cylinders, noise-pushed icospheres and a rigid-node goose rig) with
per-vertex clay colours taken from this game's own palette (render/look.js).
