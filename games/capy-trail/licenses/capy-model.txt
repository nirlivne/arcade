The capybara model (assets/capy.glb)

In-house, built by build/make_capy.py (Blender, headless Python). Not derived
from any third-party asset, CC0 library, or reference model. Proportions,
palette and pose timing are ported from this game's own S1 procedural stand-in
(render/capy.js, itself ported from the art-direction brief's
design/capy-trail/scene.js, THE-1246) -- the model sheet it matches is a
render of that same original code, not external reference art.

R3 (THE-1282, 2026-11-06): the body, head and legs were rebuilt in the same
script as lofted superellipse shapes with vertex-colour shading (darker back,
nose pad, dark feet). Still in-house, with no third-party asset. No licence-clear
rigged capybara was found that fits the clay look: the free one is a PSX-style
model under CC BY 4.0, and the rest are paid marketplace models.
