# Capy Trail

A chill capybara gallops a winding 3D forest trail, jumping logs, ducking birds and stepping lanes round
boulders. Read what's ahead, collect coins, and keep running -- one bonk ends the run.

**Status:** S1 tracer bullet (forest only). Not yet listed in `games.json`.

**Age band:** family 8+.

## How to play

- **Keyboard:** A/D or ←/→ to change lanes (or take a corner at a junction), W/↑/Space to jump,
  S/↓ to duck or belly-slide, Esc/P to pause, M to mute.
- **Touch:** a floating stick on the left half of the screen steers; JUMP and DUCK are the two pebbles on
  the right.
- The first 20 seconds are a grace period: a stumble instead of a crash, so there's time to learn the ropes.
- Your best distance is saved and shown as a flag on the trail.
