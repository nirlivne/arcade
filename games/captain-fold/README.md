# Captain Fold

A paper plane glide: hold to climb, let go to dive. Find the gaps, ride the mint air columns to refuel, and
land on the blanket at the end of the course.

**Age band:** kids 6–10. No reading needed to play — the demo and the how-to are pictures.

## How to play

- **Pull back and let go** to throw the plane from the ledge.
- **Hold anywhere to climb, let go to dive.** Speed trades for height; a constant sink pulls you down, and
  only the mint air columns give it back.
- Fly through the gaps — over the washing line, under the birdhouse, past the shed — and land on the picnic
  blanket before the house wall at the end. The closer to the bullseye, the more stars.
- **The first 20 seconds can't be lost:** a crash just bounces the paper plane back into the air. After that,
  a crash ends the flight.
- Collect **lucky stars** along good routes, and land 3 courses in a chapter to unlock the next one anyway,
  even without 3 stars.
- Clear all 24 courses across 3 chapters, or fly the **daily flight** — the same course for everyone that
  day, with its own share line.

## Controls

- Mouse, touch, or keyboard (Space / Arrow Up): hold to climb, let go to dive.
- `P` or Esc pauses; the game also pauses automatically if you switch tabs.

## Comforts

Pause (auto-pauses on tab-away), mute, a throw-arc guide toggle, instant retry, reduced motion, and a
first-20-seconds safety net (a crash bounces instead of ending the flight). Your course stars, best scores,
daily record, and unlocked paper patterns are saved automatically.

## Fold collection

Six paper patterns for the plane — Notebook (default), Graph paper (first landing), Newspaper (50 lucky
stars), Map paper (finish Chapter 1), Origami red (10 courses at 3 stars), and Gold star paper (finish
Chapter 3). Equip your favourite in the Hangar.

## Run locally

Captain Fold's game code loads as ES modules, which Chrome blocks from a plain `file://` page — serve the
folder with any static file server (for example `python -m http.server`) and open it from `http://` instead.
It works offline once loaded and can be installed as an app.
