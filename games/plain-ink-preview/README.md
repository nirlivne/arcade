# Hide in Plain Ink

A hide-and-seek spy platformer in the 60s cut-paper title-sequence style. Flip your reversible coat
to match the ink behind you, freeze, and let the Watchers look straight through you.

Level 1, "Opening Night": run along the opening titles, slip past the Watchers, solve the editor's
riddles and reach the end of the credits. Get seen and you are "snipped" (a paper gag, no harm done)
and start again from the last flag.

**Age band:** family, 10+. No death or blood.

## How to play

- Run and jump along the letters of the title sequence.
- When a Watcher's beam comes, stand in front of ink that matches your coat, flip the coat if it
  doesn't, and keep still. A hidden spy shows a dashed "cut here" outline.
- Flags save your place. Your best time and fewest snips are kept on this device.
- The first-run demo shows the moves; "How to play" on the title screen replays it any time.

## Controls

- **Move:** Arrow keys / A D
- **Jump:** Space / Up / W / Z
- **Flip the coat:** X / Shift / J
- **Pause:** Esc / P
- **Touch:** a move zone (bottom-left), JUMP + FLIP discs (bottom-right)

Works offline after the first visit, and can be installed as an app.
