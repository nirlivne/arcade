Hebrew narration voice -- Hide in Plain Ink

The six Hebrew narration lines (voice/*.mp3) were generated once, at build time, with Google
Gemini text-to-speech (tools/plain-ink-voice.mjs), then processed offline (band-pass, light
saturation, tape hiss) and encoded as MP3. The game only plays these shipped files. It never calls
Gemini or any other online service, and it holds no API key.

The generated audio is used under the Gemini API terms of service. The line texts are original to
this game (theme.js, lines.*).
